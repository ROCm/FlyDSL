# FlyDSL Newsletter #1 (inaugural)

**Subject:** FlyDSL Newsletter #1 — 2026-07-06 to 2026-09-04 (8-week lookback)

Hi all,

This is the **first FlyDSL newsletter**. Because there is no prior edition, this issue looks back **eight weeks (2026-07-06 – 2026-09-04)** — from v0.2.3 through v0.3.2, plus the 3–4 Sep landings — rather than a single bi-weekly slice. Subsequent issues will follow the AITER bi-weekly cadence. AITER Brief #2 closed on 2026-09-02; this inaugural FlyDSL issue runs two days further.

Figures are from merged PR measurements unless noted. Stack-level tok/s for vLLM / SGLang / ATOM remain in the AITER brief and are **not attributed to FlyDSL alone**.

## Executive Summary

**Over eight weeks FlyDSL went from a layout-API migration of existing kernels to a CDNA4 serving stack that can stand next to AITER ASM on FlashAttention, MXFP4 GEMM, MegaMoE, and paged decode — and the last two weeks closed the silent-wrong-answer bugs that would have made that stack unsafe to ship.**

**July – mid-August (coverage).** The public API gained a stability contract; `buffer_ops` left `expr/`; autotune landed (RMSNorm, then Softmax). Training kernels arrived (RMSNorm / LayerNorm backward, Softmax backward, optional FA LSE). MegaMoE landed on gfx950 (A8W4, then +7.8–12.3% vs the standard EP path) and later A4W4 + FP8 P2P. Dense FlashAttention on MI355X picked up D=64, short-sequence routing, XCD-local Q-block mapping (L2 hit 2.36% → 93.62%; **+20.7% / +22.6%** at S=64K / 131K vs the unmapped kernel, beating AITER ASM 5–8%), 8-wave GQA sliding-window (**1.04–1.48×** vs AITER), and bias / ALiBi / sink. A tile-programmed PA decode **matches or beats** the hand-scheduled production kernel and is faster than Gluon on every compute-bound shape (up to ~28%). MXFP6×MXFP4 and MXFP4 4-wave GEMM closed on gfx950 (4-wave within **1.2–2.6%** of AITER ASM). RDNA4 got a real `gfx120x.wmma` atom (**geomean 1.08×**); gfx1250 got MX-scaled WMMA and N-D TDM copy atoms. FP8 conv3d 8-wave reached **2.33–3.29×** vs MIOpen bf16.

**Late August – 4 Sep (trust + RDNA4).** gfx950 dense bf16 regained the XCD remap after it was default-off (**+17–19%** long-S) and ambient fastmath on `maxnumf` (**+11%**). FP8 dense attention keeps softmax mass (worst-row mean(O) 0.45 → 0.91 at S=131K) and no longer overflows int32. Lazy-rescale NaNs are gone. PA gained D192 / asymmetric K/V (**5.5–6.9×** vs Gluon V192-padded) and 64-bit cache offsets past 2 GiB. Preshuffle GEMM DMA for f16/bf16 is **+8.2%** after two A-tile bugs (silent garbage; **3.3×** slow copy) were fixed. New: RDNA3 INT8 WMMA GEMM (90–93% of gfx1100 WMMA peak; 1.23–2.00× vs `torch._int_mm`), RDNA3 f16 tile picker (up to 1.85×), gfx1250 bf16 GEMM, MXFP8 A8W8. On 3–4 Sep the GFX120X WMMA atom gained native **FP8 E4M3FN** (atom-only; 34 tests on gfx1201), optional RDNA buffer OOB checks for vLLM RDNA4 FP8 prefill, and conv dilation / `padding_mode` / groups with Flux-shape retune (**1.18–2.18×** vs prior FlyDSL; up to **2.95×** vs torch).

Shipped tags in this window: **v0.2.3 → v0.2.4 → v0.3.0 → v0.3.1 → v0.3.2** (v0.3.2 on 2026-08-27). `0.3.3` is bumped in tree, not tagged.
