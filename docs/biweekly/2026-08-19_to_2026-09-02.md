# FlyDSL Bi-weekly Brief #1

**Subject:** FlyDSL Bi-weekly Brief #1 — 2026-08-19 to 2026-09-02

Hi all,

This edition covers the **2026-08-19 – 2026-09-02** cycle (same window as AITER Brief #2). Figures below are taken from merged PR measurements unless noted. Stack-level tok/s for vLLM / SGLang / ATOM remain in the AITER brief and are **not attributed to FlyDSL alone**.

## Executive Summary

**This cycle made the gfx950 serving path trustworthy again — silent NaNs, dropped FP8 softmax mass, partial A-tile GEMM garbage, and >2 GiB paged-cache wrap are closed — and expanded native GEMM / MoE / conv coverage on gfx950, gfx1250, and RDNA3 so those platforms no longer have to sit on torch, hipBLASLt, or MIOpen for the shapes we measured.**

Dense bf16 FlashAttention on MI355X regained its XCD workgroup remap (+17.4% / +19.4% at S=180K / 240K, bit-identical) and ambient fastmath on `fx.maxnumf` (11.1–11.9% at long S; 29 call sites across attention, PA, MLA, MoE). FP8 dense attention now keeps softmax mass on trained peaked scores (worst-row mean(O) 0.45 → 0.91 at S=131K; ~3% throughput) and no longer overflows int32 when `B*S*H*D ≥ 2^31`. Paged attention gained D192 query staging, 64-bit cache offsets past 2 GiB, and BF16 / asymmetric K/V (MiMo-V2.5-Pro Q/K 192, V 128 without padding V to 192): 5.5–6.9× vs AITER Gluon V192-padded at bs32 / qlen4 / ctx 16K–64K on gfx950. PA metadata decode is 1.19× geomean on MI308X (11 of 12 shapes; up to 28.8%). Preshuffle GEMM on gfx950 now DMA-copies f16/bf16 A tiles (+8.2% to 1499 TF/s at M=54560, N=K=8192) after two A-tile bugs were fixed (silent wrong results on non-multiple tiles; 3.3× slow whole-tile A copy at `128×256×256`). New kernels this cycle: RDNA3 INT8 WMMA GEMM (90–93% of the gfx1100 WMMA issue ceiling; 1.23–2.00× vs `torch._int_mm` on 15/17 shapes, up to 4.33× skinny-M; median 1.36× vs Triton 3.8), RDNA3 f16 shape-aware tiles (1.2–1.85× below 1024³), gfx1250 bf16 GEMM plus TDM prefetch, MXFP8 A8W8 (block-scale and MX 1×32) on the preshuffle path, MegaMoE A4W4 + blockwise FP8 P2P on 8×MI355X DeepSeek-V4 Pro, and FP8 conv3d 8-wave (2.33–3.29× vs MIOpen bf16 e2e, up to 2565 TF kernel-only on MI355X). Softmax is the second opt-in autotune adopter after RMSNorm; default `BLOCK_THREADS=256` is unchanged. FlyDSL **v0.3.2** shipped 2026-08-27; `0.3.3` is bumped in tree and not yet tagged.

## AITER Integration (brief)

AITER is the kernel aggregation layer that consumes FlyDSL; relevant landings on the AITER side in the same window:

- FlyDSL radix-select top-K decode is the AITER path on gfx950/gfx942: **1.1×–3.9×** lower latency vs HIP (K∈{512–4096}, context up to 1M); 29/29 correctness tests. — [aiter#5011](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/5011)
- FlyDSL decode top-K dispatch was then **silently disabled** by a stale module reference and restored on gfx942/gfx950. — [aiter#5210](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/5210)

vLLM and SGLang continue to take FlyDSL through AITER. Nightly workflows: `flydsl-vllm-integration.yaml`, `flydsl-sglang-integration.yaml` (MI355×8, `GPU_ARCH=gfx950`).
