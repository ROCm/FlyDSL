# M13 native A8W4 EP8 acceptance baseline (MI355X)

Validated 2026-08-27 on eight MI355X GPUs. The production contract is M13
`D=3584, I=1280, E=384, EP=8, topk=8`, with `tokens=MTPR` in
`1,4,8,16,32,64,128,256,512`. MTPR 1024 is intentionally rejected by the
current decode contract.

The gate uses 20 CUDAGraph replays after ten warmups and compares the maximum
latency across all eight ranks. Accuracy uses the dequantized-weight oracle;
graph replay output must have relative L2 below `1e-6`. Performance must be no
more than 5% above the committed rank-max baseline.

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| 4 | 0.04782 | 73.7 | 22.2 | 101.9 | 97.8 |
| 8 | 0.04769 | 70.7 | 29.1 | 107.1 | 109.3 |
| 16 | 0.04712 | 97.2 | 32.6 | 134.2 | 137.0 |

All nine cases passed, with `graphReplayRelL2max=0` and zero strict failures.
